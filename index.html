How it works: a word is spoken aloud — type how it's spelled within 30 seconds. The clock never stops, even while you use a lifeline. You get three lifelines per game: 📖 Definition, 🔤 First & last letter and 🔢 Word length. Each can be used once. Not sure what you heard? Switch between American, British and Indian pronunciation during the game. Words are never repeated — not within a game, and not in future games on this device. Fresh words are fetched from a free online dictionary, so an internet connection gives you far more words.
